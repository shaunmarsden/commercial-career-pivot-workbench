# Fictional Pivot Output

This worked example uses only the [fictional CV](fictional-commercial-cv.md). It demonstrates the method, not a recommendation or employment outcome.

## Initial Role Hypotheses

| Role | Why it may fit | Evidence | Gap or unknown | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer Success Manager | Account reviews, renewals and stakeholder coordination transfer directly | Portfolio management, quarterly reviews, renewal coordination | Product-adoption and retention metrics are not shown | Strong |
| Sales Enablement Specialist | Coaching and repeatable sales methods may transfer | Coached new colleagues, introduced a review template | No evidence of designing a wider programme | Plausible |
| Partnerships Manager | Commercial conversations and internal coordination may transfer | Discovery, proposals, cross-functional work | No direct partner-channel evidence | Plausible |
| Commercial Operations Analyst | CRM and forecasting experience are relevant | CRM management, weekly forecasts | No evidence of analytics depth or systems ownership | Speculative |
| Implementation Consultant | Discovery, workshops and handover may transfer | Workshop facilitation, stakeholder coordination | No delivery or technical implementation evidence | Speculative |

## What the CV Can and Cannot Show

The CV supports adjacent-role hypotheses, but it cannot establish hiring demand, likely pay or entry difficulty. Those questions require current job descriptions and market sources.

It also does not support claims of:

- Direct people management
- Programme ownership
- Advanced analytics
- Technical implementation
- Partnership-channel experience

## Shortlist for Testing

### 1. Customer Success Manager

**Why test it:** The strongest direct overlap is account ownership, reviews, renewals and stakeholder coordination.

**What to check:** Whether current roles require product-adoption metrics, technical depth or a different type of portfolio experience.

### 2. Sales Enablement Specialist

**Why test it:** Coaching and improving a repeatable account-review method may be useful evidence.

**What to check:** Whether current roles expect learning design, programme ownership or enablement technology experience.

### 3. Partnerships Manager

**Why test it:** The commercial and cross-functional skills may transfer.

**What to check:** Whether direct channel experience is essential or merely preferred.

## 30-Day Learning Plan

### Week 1

- Collect five current job descriptions for each shortlisted role
- Mark every requirement as supported, partly supported, unsupported or unclear
- Record salary and location claims separately rather than asking AI to estimate them

### Week 2

- Ask one trusted person in each relevant field to challenge the role assumptions
- Do not ask for a referral or automated introduction
- Update the evidence map with what was misunderstood

### Week 3

- Build one small portfolio artefact for the most repeated, learnable gap
- Rewrite the professional summary for one role and run the CV claim check

### Week 4

- Decide whether to pursue, test further, build towards, park or reject each role
- Record the evidence behind the decision

## Honest Conclusion

Customer Success Manager is the strongest hypothesis from the supplied evidence. Sales Enablement Specialist and Partnerships Manager are worth testing. The other options should not be presented as strong matches without more evidence.

No salary, demand or remote-work claim has been made because no live market research was supplied.
