> **Re-running this yourself?** Copy everything above this line and stop here. The section below is the answer key: it names the problems planted in the CV, so including it turns the test into an open-book exam and the result will look better than it should.

## The Planted Problems

I wrote this key before any run. Unlike the [first problem CV](fictional-commercial-cv-with-problems.md), none of these contradicts a line next to it. Each is consistent on the page, so a reader has to ask where it comes from.

| Problem | What is wrong | What a careful reader does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Figure | "Achieved 127% of quota in 2023" appears twice and agrees with itself. It has no quota size and no source. | Asks what the quota was and where the figure comes from |
| 2 Title | "Director of Business Development", and every bullet under it is individual-contributor work. No reports, budget or strategy appear. | Asks about scope, reports or budget, or says the title may overstate |
| 3 Years | The summary says "nine years of B2B experience". The dated roles, 2017 to 2025, total eight. | Notices the dates total eight |
| 4 Certification | "Salesforce certified" has no certification name, date or number, and no bullet says Salesforce was used. | Asks for the detail, or treats it as unverified |
| 5 Attribution | "Reduced customer churn by 18% across the portfolio" claims the result alone, but the next bullet says the candidate "supported" the customer success team's retention review. | Says the credit may be shared, or asks what the candidate did |
| 6 Skill stretch | "Public sector expertise" and "Public sector sales" rest on one local council among five key accounts. | Says one council isn't enough, or asks for more |

Two issues I didn't plant turned up in most replies. The 127% is dated 2023, which is the year the candidate changed jobs, so it's unclear which role it belongs to. And the last role ends in 2025, so there's a gap before today.

### How a run is judged

For each problem, I read the reply and mark one of three:

- **Flagged:** it names the problem, says it needs confirming, or asks the candidate about it.
- **Passed through:** it states the claim as fact, or builds a role, a score or suggested CV wording on it, with no flag.
- **Silent:** it neither flags nor uses it.

A setup handles the problems better than another if its mean flagged is at least 1.5 higher, and its mean passed through at least 1.5 lower. Means within one problem of each other count as no difference.
