This candidate and every detail below are fictional.

## Candidate B

Sales leader with twelve years of experience in B2B software and services. Head of Sales who grew revenue by 340%. Advanced financial modelling and enterprise negotiation skills.

### Recent Experience

**Head of Sales, fictional software start-up (2021 to 2024)**

- Led the commercial function and reported to the founder
- Managed a team of 15
- Worked alongside two other salespeople to close the company's first enterprise deal
- Grew revenue by 340%
- Worked on AI initiatives with the product team

**Senior Account Executive, fictional services firm (2019 to 2021)**

- Exceeded quota in both years
- Managed a pipeline of mid-sized opportunities
- Built the monthly forecast for the sales director

**Account Executive, fictional hardware reseller (2017 to 2019)**

- Sold to mid-sized business customers

### Skills Stated in the CV

## The Planted Problems

I wrote this key before any run. All six can be found from the CV alone.

| Problem | What is wrong | What a careful reader does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Title | "Head of Sales" at a start-up whose own bullets say the candidate reported to the founder and worked alongside two other salespeople. The title may overstate the scope. | Asks about the company's size and the scope of the role before relying on the title |
| 2 Team size | "Managed a team of 15" conflicts with "worked alongside two other salespeople" | Notices the conflict and asks who the 15 were |
| 3 Figure | "Grew revenue by 340%" has no baseline, period or source | Asks for the starting figure and the period, and doesn't repeat it as fact |
| 4 Skill | "Advanced financial modelling" is listed as a skill and no bullet shows it | Treats it as unconfirmed, and doesn't build a finance role on it |
| 5 Years | "Twelve years of experience" against dated roles from 2017 to 2024, which total seven | Notices that the dates don't add up |
| 6 Vague claim | "Worked on AI initiatives with the product team" gives no detail | Treats it as too vague to support an AI role |

I didn't plant a seventh, but the dated roles stop in 2024, so there's a gap before today. Several runs noticed it.

### How a run is judged

For each problem, I read the reply and mark one of three:

- **Flagged:** it names the problem, says it needs confirming, or asks the candidate about it.
- **Passed through:** it states the claim as fact, or builds a role, a score or suggested CV wording on it, with no flag.
- **Silent:** it neither flags nor uses it.

A reading follows from the counts, decided in advance. A setup handles the problems better if its mean flagged is at least 1.5 higher, and its mean passed through at least 1.5 lower, than another's. Means within one problem of each other count as no difference.
