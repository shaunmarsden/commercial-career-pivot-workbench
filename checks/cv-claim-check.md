# CV Claim Check

Use this before accepting a rewritten summary, experience section or application answer.

## Check Every Claim

For each statement, ask:

1. Where is the supporting evidence in the source CV or confirmed notes?
2. Does the wording increase the level of responsibility?
3. Has a contribution become sole ownership?
4. Has a task become an achievement without a result?
5. Has a related skill become direct experience?
6. Has an estimate become a precise number?
7. Has industry language changed the underlying meaning?

## Labels

- **Supported:** directly evidenced and fairly described
- **Needs confirmation:** plausible, but the source is incomplete
- **Overstated:** based on real experience but stronger than the evidence
- **Invented:** not supported by the supplied information

Only supported claims should enter a finished CV. Confirm uncertain facts with the person rather than guessing.

## Final Questions

- Could the person explain this statement clearly in an interview?
- Is the achievement still accurate without impressive adjectives?
- Are dates, figures and responsibilities consistent throughout?
- Has any confidential customer or employer information been exposed?
