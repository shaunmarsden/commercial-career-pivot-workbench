# Market Claim Check

Use this whenever an output makes claims about salary, hiring demand, remote work, how hard a role is to get into, or growth.

## Minimum Evidence

A useful claim should include:

- A link to a current source
- The date you looked at it
- Country or region
- Role level
- Salary basis and currency, where relevant
- How many listings or records you reviewed
- Important limits

Several current job listings and a trusted labour-market source beat a single salary website or an AI's estimate.

## Warning Signs

- An exact salary range with no location or date
- "High demand" with no sample or source
- A national figure used for a local decision
- Senior and junior roles lumped together
- Advertised salary mistaken for the likely offer
- Remote work assumed from old listings
- One unusual vacancy treated as typical

## Status Labels

- Verified: supported by several relevant, current sources
- Limited evidence: some current evidence, but the sample or scope is weak
- Not yet verified: no good enough current evidence
- Conflicting: relevant sources point different ways

Don't cover a gap with confident wording. Keep the uncertainty visible.
