# Market Claim Check

Use this whenever an output makes claims about salary, hiring demand, remote work, entry difficulty or growth.

## Minimum Evidence

A useful claim should include:

- A current source link
- The date accessed
- Country or region
- Role level
- Salary basis and currency, where relevant
- Number of listings or records reviewed
- Important limitations

Prefer several current job listings and an authoritative labour-market source over a single salary website or an AI-generated estimate.

## Warning Signs

- A precise salary range with no location or date
- “High demand” with no sample or source
- A national figure used for a local decision
- Senior and junior roles combined
- Advertised salary confused with likely offer
- Remote availability inferred from old listings
- One unusual vacancy treated as typical

## Status Labels

- **Verified:** supported by multiple relevant, current sources
- **Limited evidence:** some current evidence, but the sample or scope is weak
- **Not yet verified:** no adequate current evidence
- **Conflicting:** relevant sources point in different directions

Do not fill a gap with confidence. Keep the uncertainty visible.
