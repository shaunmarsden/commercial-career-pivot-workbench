# Commercial Career Pivot Workbench

AI can suggest fifteen possible jobs from a CV in seconds. That does not mean all fifteen are realistic, in demand or worth pursuing.

This project is an evidence-checked workflow for sales and commercial professionals exploring an adjacent role or a wider career change. It helps turn a CV into a shortlist of role hypotheses, shows what evidence supports each one and separates useful ideas from claims that need current market research.

> AI generates and organises possibilities. The person checks the evidence and decides what to pursue.

## What It Produces

- A longlist of plausible roles, including adjacent options
- A shortlist ranked by evidence of fit, not confidence of wording
- A map from each role requirement to experience already demonstrated
- Clear gaps split into essential, learnable and uncertain
- A list of salary and demand claims that still need live checking
- A small 30-day plan to test the strongest options

## The Workflow

1. **Protect:** remove unnecessary personal, customer and employer information.
2. **Extract:** turn the CV into an evidence map without rewriting it yet.
3. **Generate:** create role hypotheses and explain why each might fit.
4. **Check:** compare the best roles with real job descriptions.
5. **Research:** verify demand, salary and location claims using current sources.
6. **Test:** choose small actions that produce better evidence before a major decision.
7. **Decide:** let the person choose what to pursue, pause or reject.

## What This Is Not

- A guarantee that someone qualifies for a role
- A source of live salary or hiring data
- A replacement for reading current job descriptions
- A reason to invent achievements, seniority or industry experience
- A place to upload or publish a real CV
- Automated job applications or unsolicited messages

## Safe Use

Use an AI tool your organisation permits. Remove contact details and any customer, employer or confidential information that is not required for the task. Keep real CVs and completed evidence maps private.

Everything under `examples/` is fictional. The repository contains no real candidate, employer, customer or contact data.
